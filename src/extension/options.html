Velqra capture

Velqra handles supported, unauthenticated GET downloads when its app is ready. Other downloads stay in Chrome. Files that need cookies or authorization stay in Chrome.

Choose On, Per-site only, or Off in the app's settings. This page cannot change app settings.

When using another download manager, choose one manager or separate browser profiles to avoid conflicts. Velqra never disables another extension.

Click Velqra's toolbar icon to keep the next top-frame download in that tab in Chrome (60 seconds). Click again to clear. The bypass resets when the worker restarts.
